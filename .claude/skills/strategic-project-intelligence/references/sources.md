# Source Reference

Generic categories — substitute the actual outlets relevant to the subject's country/sector. Example below uses a Greek infrastructure project.

## Official / Primary

| Source type | Example (Greek infrastructure) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Subject's own newsroom/press page | Company or authority's official site (e.g. `aia.gr` newsroom for Athens International Airport) | Highest-weight source; cite the release title and date |
| Regulator / concession authority | Ministry press releases, regulatory body statements | Relevant for PPP/concession projects, licensing, approvals |
| Parent company / shareholder disclosures | Annual reports, investor presentations, stock-exchange filings if listed or if a parent company is | Best source for hard financial/capex numbers |

## Domestic Press (check first)

- Naftemporiki (naftemporiki.gr) — business/financial
- Kathimerini Οικονομία (kathimerini.gr) — business/financial
- Capital.gr — business/financial
- Reporter.gr — business/financial
- insider.gr — business
- Sector trade press (e.g. for aviation: specialized Greek aviation/transport outlets)

Query pattern: `site:[outlet] [subject name] [keyword: επέκταση/expansion, καθυστέρηση/delay, επενδύσεις/investment]`

## International Press

- Reuters, Bloomberg — for financially material events
- Sector trade publications (e.g. Construction Europe, Airport Technology, ACI Europe/ACI World for aviation infrastructure; adapt to the subject's sector)

Query pattern: `[subject name] [keyword] site:[outlet]` or `"[subject name]" news [current year]`

## Periodic Reports

- Annual report / integrated report (usually PDF on the subject's or parent's investor relations page)
- Quarterly results or traffic/production statistics releases
- Analyst or rating agency notes, if publicly summarized in press

Prioritize numbers from these over press paraphrasing — press often rounds or simplifies figures from the primary report.

## Search Hygiene

- Restrict to the period since the last briefing (default: past 7 days for a weekly cadence) except when reporting a quarterly/annual filing, which is checked against the subject's known reporting calendar regardless of the 7-day window
- Cite outlet + headline + date for every claim — a strategic briefing without sources is not actionable
- Distinguish confirmed facts (official announcement, filed report) from speculation/analyst opinion (press "sources say," rumor) — label the latter explicitly
